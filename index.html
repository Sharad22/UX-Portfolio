About me

I design with empathy & intent.

Hi, I'm Sharadindu — a UX Designer and Product Thinker with a belief that great design is invisible. I work at the intersection of user psychology, business logic, and visual craft.

My work spans mobile apps, web platforms, and design systems. I've helped startups launch products from zero and helped companies rethink broken experiences from the ground up.

I approach every project as a problem to be understood first — not a screen to be designed. Research, clarity, and iteration are the tools I trust most.
