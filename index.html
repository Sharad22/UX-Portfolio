About me

Designing experiences
people actually remember.

Hi, I'm Sharadindu — a UX Designer who believes the best interfaces are the ones that get out of the way and let people do what they came to do.

I work at the intersection of user psychology, business goals, and visual clarity. My process starts with deep research and ends with designs that are not just beautiful — but purposeful and tested.

From early-stage startups to redesigning established products, I've tackled everything from mobile apps and SaaS dashboards to landing pages and design systems. Every project teaches me something new about how people think, click, and feel.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably reverse-engineering why some apps feel effortless and others feel like a chore — and taking notes.

My process

How I work.

01
Discover

User interviews, competitive audits, and stakeholder alignment. I listen more than I design early on.

02
Define

Synthesising research into clear problem statements, personas, and opportunity maps. Evidence over assumptions.

03
Design

From rough sketches to high-fidelity Figma. Prototype early, test often — not just at the end.

04

Handoff-ready specs, design systems, and post-launch measurement. Design doesn't stop at delivery.
